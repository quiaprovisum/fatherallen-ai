About

Fr. Kenneth Allen

Priest, writer, musician, photographer, baker — between assignments, still at the work.

Fr. Kenneth Allen is a Catholic priest of the Archdiocese of New Orleans. After twenty-two years in pastoral ministry, he is currently between parish assignments while recovering and writing.

Sacred music, photography, and baking have long been part of his life — companions to the work of prayer, not a brand. He writes as a pilgrim: about Scripture, spirituality, ordinary days, and the slow work of grace.

Most of the writing lives on Substack, under Reflections and Rabbit Holes — including homilies offered as a gift there. This site is the front door and identity desk: a place for a longer featured piece when one is ready, refreshed by swapping that essay rather than by daily posting.

The Catholic Kitchen is a forthcoming food shop, separate from the gift writing. It will live at its own home when the time comes; recipes and shop goods will not be hosted here.

For prayer requests and notes, see Contact. For ongoing reflections, visit Substack.
