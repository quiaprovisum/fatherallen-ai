September 29 ◆ The Feast of St. Michael

Summer lets go.
The season turns.

A cool front moves through. Michael stands watch.

The first cool air can make the whole country feel newly awake. This week, the Feast of St. Michael meets that change of season: a small threshold between what is passing and what is beginning.

About the experiment

A small project about people and AI.

FatherAllen.ai is an ongoing exploration of what artificial intelligence can—and cannot—contribute to Catholic life, reflection, and creativity. We’ll make small experiments, look closely at what they offer, and learn as we go.

At the turn of the season

A feast for the threshold.

The old warmth loosens its hold. A cool front crosses the land, and the air asks us to notice what has changed. Michaelmas arrives in that in-between: summer not quite gone, autumn not yet settled.

Saint Michael's feast turns our attention toward protection and courage. It is a good day to pause at the threshold and remember that we do not cross it alone.

Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle; be our protection against the wickedness and snares of the devil. — Prayer to St. Michael
